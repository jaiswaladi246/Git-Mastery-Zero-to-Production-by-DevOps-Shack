# A06 · Choose a Branching Strategy

**Level:** Intermediate · **Estimated time:** 2 hours · **Related:** [Module 11](../modules/README.md)

## Objective

Recommend and justify a branching strategy for three different companies.

## Requirements

1. Company 1: 8-person SaaS startup deploying several times a day.
2. Company 2: mobile banking app, bi-weekly store releases, regulator sign-off.
3. Company 3: open-source database supporting the last 3 minor versions.
4. For each, give the strategy, a gitGraph diagram, hotfix flow, release flow, tooling (flags, CI, protections), and the main risk with mitigation.

## Deliverables

- STRATEGY.md (max 4 pages)

## Grading rubric

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Fit to context | 40 |
| Diagrams | 20 |
| Hotfix/release flows correct | 25 |
| Risks and mitigations | 15 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Stretch goal

Add a migration plan for moving Company 2 from Git Flow to trunk-based with release branches.
